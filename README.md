# littlEye AI — forensic histology AI, trained on-device (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

**littlEye AI** lets forensic pathologists, pathologists and medical students train their own
Core ML models from H&E slide microphotographs — entirely on the device, with no internet and no
telemetry. Dataset across 27 organs and tissues, colleague review with an audit trail, an
independent test set with confusion matrix, an atlas of 103 diagnoses with citations, and
AES-GCM-encrypted `.le3AI` model / `.le3set` dataset sharing. **Free since version 3.0.**

- Product page (SK / EN): **https://forensika.eu/littleye-ai/** · https://forensika.eu/en/littleye-ai/
- App Store (free; iOS/iPadOS 17+, macOS 14+): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6799417580
- Manuals (SK/EN), "how it learns", data sources: this site → https://dzanino.github.io/littlEyeAI/
- Part of the **Forensika** family of offline forensic-medicine apps: https://forensika.eu/

Developed in collaboration with three departments of the Faculty of Medicine, Comenius University
in Bratislava (Histology and Embryology; Pathological Anatomy; Forensic Medicine and Forensic
Toxicology).

Keywords: littlEye AI histológia, offline forenzná aplikácia, AI v patológii, tréning modelu
v zariadení · forensic histology AI on-device, on-device Core ML histology training, digital

Author: Ján Šikuta, MD, PhD · forensika.eu@icloud.com. Research and decision-support tool for
qualified professionals — not a medical device; it does not provide diagnoses.
